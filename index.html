Emergency Preparedness Center

Basic reminders for common hazards. Always follow the school emergency plan and instructions from authorities.

〰

Earthquake

  1. DROP, COVER, and HOLD ON.
  2. Protect your head and neck.
  3. Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  4. When shaking stops, evacuate calmly if instructed.
  5. Expect aftershocks and follow the designated assembly procedure.
☂

Typhoon / Severe Weather

  1. Monitor official weather and local advisories.
  2. Stay indoors and away from floodwater.
  3. Keep emergency supplies and charged devices ready.
  4. Follow class suspension and evacuation announcements.
  5. Never cross moving floodwater.
♨

Fire

  1. Raise the alarm and notify responsible adults.
  2. Evacuate using the designated safe route.
  3. Stay low if there is smoke.
  4. Never return to a burning building.
  5. Use a fire extinguisher only if trained, safe, and with a clear exit.
✚

Medical Emergency

  1. Alert the teacher or designated first aider.
  2. Keep the area safe and give the person space.
  3. Do not move a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.
  4. Request qualified medical assistance.
  5. Share only necessary information with responders.

LEARN • PRACTICE • PREPARE

DRRM Learning Hub

Short learning topics for learners, parents, teachers, and community members.

01

Know your hazards

Learn about hazards that may affect the school and community, including strong winds, flooding, earthquakes, and fire.

Suggested activity: Draw a simple hazard map with an adult.
02

Prepare an emergency go-bag

Discuss safe drinking water, ready-to-eat food, flashlight, whistle, basic first-aid supplies, and important contact details.

Suggested activity: Make a family preparedness checklist.
03

Know your safe places

Ask your teacher or barangay officials about approved evacuation areas and safe routes.

Suggested activity: Practice the approved evacuation route with supervision.
04

Help without putting yourself at risk

Stay calm, inform a trusted adult, follow instructions, and never enter unsafe areas to rescue belongings.

Suggested activity: Role-play how to report an emergency to an adult.
