Quick safety guides

Earthquake
  1. DROP, COVER, and HOLD ON.
  2. Protect your head and neck; stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  3. When shaking stops, follow the school's evacuation instructions.
  4. Proceed to the designated assembly area and await further instructions.
Typhoon and flooding
  1. Monitor official weather and local advisories.
  2. Stay away from rivers, streams, and floodwater.
  3. Prepare essential supplies and follow evacuation announcements.
  4. Never walk or drive through moving floodwater.
Fire
  1. Raise the alarm and notify responsible adults.
  2. Evacuate using the designated safe route.
  3. Stay low if there is smoke and never return to a burning building.
  4. Use firefighting equipment only if trained and it is safe to do so.
Medical emergency
  1. Alert the teacher or designated first aider.
  2. Keep the area safe and request qualified medical assistance.
  3. Do not move a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.
  4. Follow instructions from trained responders.
Content validation required

School-specific routes, maps, contact details, and procedures must be checked and approved by designated school personnel and relevant partner agencies before public release.

ABOUT THE INNOVATION

PROJECT SAGIP-LOBO

School Action for Disaster Preparedness and Integrated Protection

Purpose

PROJECT SAGIP-LOBO uses a centralized digital platform as its primary information hub while maintaining printed and downloadable offline materials so essential information remains available when electricity, internet connectivity, or mobile signals are interrupted.

The platform organizes school DRRM activities, verified emergency contact information, hazard-specific safety guidance, school hazard and evacuation information, emergency procedures, preparedness resources, and learning materials.

Five connected components

  1. SAGIP-INFO – centralized disaster information hub.
  2. SAGIP-ALERTO – emergency contacts, communication guidance, and response procedures.
  3. SAGIP-BANTAY – school hazard awareness, mapping, and student-led safety reporting.
  4. SAGIP-KAAGAPAY – family and community preparedness and coordination.
  5. SAGIP-SIGURADO – monitoring, evaluation, and continuous improvement.

Important limitations

This platform does not replace official emergency response systems, established school protocols, or instructions from authorized authorities. Sensitive personal information must not be publicly displayed. Emergency information must be validated by designated school personnel and, where applicable, relevant partner agencies.
